Who We Are

About GTE

Graham's Trucking and Excavation is a third-generation family business based in Hebron, Indiana. We've been moving dirt in this region for decades — digging basements, putting in water and sewer lines, grading lots, and hauling material.

We run our own equipment and do the work ourselves. When you call, you're talking to the people who will actually be on your job — and we show up when we say we will.

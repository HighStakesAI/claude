## 2. LICENSING, INSURANCE & CREDENTIALS

**Read this before you skip it.** In Florida, the registration or certification number of
a contractor must appear in *every* offer of services, business proposal, bid, contract,
or advertisement, in any medium (Fla. Stat. §489.119(5)(b)). Your website is an
advertisement. If we build you a site without your license number on it, we have put you
out of compliance and exposed you to a notice of noncompliance and fines. Most other
states have a comparable rule. We will not publish a contractor site without this.
